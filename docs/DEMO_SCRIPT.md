# KAAMPROOF — 2-3 MINUTE LIVE DEMONSTRATION SCRIPT
**Competition:** VibeX Codes 2026 — Track 1: Daily Workers & Informal Trades  
**Synthetic Scenario:** Electrician switchboard inspection, repair & customer sign-off  

---

## Act 1: The Problem Setup (0:00 – 0:35)

> **Speaker:**
> *"Every day across India, millions of electricians, plumbers, and technicians coordinate jobs over phone calls. A customer asks to fix a tripping MCB. The electrician quotes ₹3,200, takes ₹1,000 advance for parts, and finishes the job. But at billing, the customer argues: 'I only agreed to ₹2,000', or 'You haven't shown me what you replaced', or disputes whether an advance was paid.
> 
> Without written proof, technicians face payment delays, distrust, and disputes.
> 
> Meet **KaamProof** — a lightweight job-accountability protocol. Every job agreed. Every payment tracked. Every task documented."*

---

## Act 2: Job Agreement Creation & Customer Confirmation (0:35 – 1:15)

> **Speaker:**
> *"Here is Vikram, an electrician. He creates an agreement on his phone in 30 seconds.
> 
> 1. He selects **Electrical**, enters customer **Ramesh Sharma**, sets the address, and specifies the scope: *'Main Distribution Board rewiring, replace burnt neutral bar, install 40A DP isolator'*.
> 2. He enters the agreed price: **₹3,200**, records a **₹1,000 UPI advance**, and clicks **Create & Generate Link**.
> 3. KaamProof generates a private, unique customer link. No app download needed for the customer.
> 4. Ramesh opens the link on WhatsApp, sees the exact scope, parts, price, and the ₹1,000 advance acknowledged. He clicks **Accept & Confirm Job Agreement**. Both parties are now protected."*

---

## Act 3: Evidence Logging & Customer Completion Sign-Off (1:15 – 1:55)

> **Speaker:**
> *"Now on-site:
> 1. Before opening the switchboard, Vikram snaps a photo of the burnt neutral busbar and uploads it directly to the job ledger.
> 2. He completes the repair, installs the genuine Havells isolator, and uploads the completion photo.
> 3. He clicks **Request Customer Sign-off**.
> 4. Ramesh refreshes his confirmation page. He sees the before and after photos. Satisfied, he clicks **Approve Work Completion**.
> 5. Notice: If Ramesh had an issue, he could raise a disagreement with a mandatory reason — KaamProof never automatically marks disputed jobs as approved."*

---

## Act 4: Deterministic Payment Settlement & Job Receipt (1:55 – 2:30)

> **Speaker:**
> *"With work approved, the remaining balance is calculated deterministically: ₹3,200 minus ₹1,000 advance leaves exactly ₹2,200 due.
> 
> 1. Vikram records the final ₹2,200 payment via UPI.
> 2. Outstanding balance drops to ₹0. The job status transitions to **Settled & Closed**.
> 3. Vikram clicks **Print Job Receipt / Summary** to produce a clean, verifiable job certificate with complete photo evidence and payment ledger.
> 
> KaamProof transforms informal verbal chaos into verifiable professional trust — without complex software or expensive hardware."*
