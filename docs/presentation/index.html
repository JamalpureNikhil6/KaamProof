Problem Hypothesis

The Informal Trade Dilemma

Verbal coordination leads to regular payment friction

  • 90%+ Informal Coordination: Tradesmen coordinate jobs over quick voice calls and informal chats.
  • Lost Details & Scope Creep: Customers forget verbal quotes or claim additional work was included for free.
  • Unacknowledged Advances: Cash or UPI advance payments for parts are unrecorded and disputed later.
  • Completion Disagreements: Customers withhold final payment claiming the issue wasn't properly resolved.
The Cost of Ambiguity:

Informal technicians routinely lose 15% to 30% of their earned revenue or experience weeks of delayed payment due to avoidable verbal disputes. Neither customer nor worker has an immutable source of truth.

Proposed Solution

The KaamProof Protocol

A lightweight accountability layer that creates mutual protection

  • Zero Customer App Install: Customers open a secure unique web link on WhatsApp without login or friction.
  • Upfront 1-Click Confirmation: Explicit customer acceptance of price, scope, and parts.
  • Before & After Photo Evidence: Visual proof attached directly to the job timeline.
  • Completion Sign-Off / Dispute Flow: Customer inspects work and signs off, or records exact dispute reason.
Key Design Invariant:

Disputed jobs are never automatically declared approved. Both parties retain dignity, transparency, and a permanent audit trail.

Security & Boundaries

Reliability, Privacy & Limitations

Honest delivery reporting without fabricated claims

Guaranteed Invariants:

  • Disputed jobs halt completion until resolved.
  • Payments cannot exceed the balance due.
  • Zero personal tracking or third-party cookies.
  • Minimal PII (name, address, optional contact).

Known Engineering Boundaries:

  • Local Storage: Filesystem JSON database (no multi-region distributed clustering).
  • Synthetic Payments: We do not simulate fake UPI webhooks; payments are recorded by the worker as cash/UPI receipts.
  • Zero Fabricated Data: No made-up user interviews or artificial adoption percentages.
